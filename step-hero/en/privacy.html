Step Hero Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026　|　Game design: Shao Chu Chen

In short

  • Your daily step records stay on your iPhone. Only when you're signed in to Game Center is your weekly total of steps claimed in the game sent to the Weekly Steps leaderboard.
  • For leaderboards and friend battles, your game character data is shared through Apple's Game Center and iCloud.
  • We don't run our own servers, we don't collect your name, phone number or location, and we never sell any data.
  • You can choose to watch ads for rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and only non-personalized ads are shown.

1. Step data

With your permission, the app reads your step count through the iPhone's "Motion & Fitness" feature and turns it into in-game gold, gacha tickets and EXP. Step records are stored on your device; we have no servers of our own and never sell them to anyone. If you're signed in to Game Center, your weekly total of steps claimed in the game is sent to the "Weekly Steps" leaderboard (see section 3). You can turn this off at any time under "Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness" on your iPhone.

2. Game saves

Your progress — level, gear, gold and so on — is stored on your device and backed up automatically to your own private iCloud space (Apple's CloudKit private database). Only your Apple Account can see this backup; we cannot read it. On a new phone or after reinstalling the app, sign in with the same Apple Account to restore your progress. If you're not signed in to iCloud, progress is stored on the device only.

3. Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends your power, weekly steps (steps claimed in the game) and arena rating to Apple Game Center leaderboards. Other players can see your Game Center name and scores. Game Center is provided by Apple and is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Hero Arena (iCloud public database)

So that friends and other players can challenge your clone, the app stores the following in Apple's iCloud CloudKit public database:

  • Your Game Center player identifier (a number Apple generates for this game — not your Apple Account or email address)
  • Your in-game character name, level, power and arena rating
  • Your character's stats, gear, skills and appearance
  • Battle records (who challenged whom, the result and rating changes)

Other players can see this data. It is used only for in-game battles. Please don't use your real name or personal information as your character name.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment information — only whether a purchase succeeded.

6. Ads

In the game you can choose to "watch an ad" for extra rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only, we don't ask to track your activity across other apps or websites, and we don't show a tracking permission prompt. Google may collect device information (such as device model, operating system, approximate region and ad interactions) to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required (for example, in Europe), the app will ask for your consent first, and you can change it anytime under "Settings > Ad Privacy Settings" in the game.

This game does not use any third-party analytics tools.

7. Children

This game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes your local save. To delete your clone and battle records in the cloud, tell us your in-game character name through the contact method below and we'll help.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If you have any questions, please leave a message on our GitHub issues page.
