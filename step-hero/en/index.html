FAQ

My steps aren't increasing? On your iPhone, go to "Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness" and make sure Step Hero is allowed.

Can't see the leaderboards or the arena? Sign in under "Settings > Game Center" on your iPhone, and make sure you're signed in to iCloud.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone? Your progress is backed up automatically to your own iCloud. On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account to restore it.

How do I change the language? In the game, go to "Settings > Language" and choose 繁體中文, English or 日本語.
